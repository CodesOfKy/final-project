RAISE A CARNIVORE PLANT


This is mostly a prototyping. As you can see in the video the time intervals only lasts a few seconds. The plant can be dragged around too, along with the objects around it. There are growth cycles that starts with a leaf, medium plant and adult. The leaves will overgrow and can be removed with a click. A thirst and hunger meter is in the backend but I have not added those to the UI. You can take care of the plant by feeding it with meat and watering it.

The sprites are all drawn by me with the use of pixilart.com website. I have learned how to draw more sprites, including the growth stages of the sprite. I have also learned how to draw background for the environment of the game. The intention is to make a warm cozy background with wood texture of the windows and table.

WHEN I FIRST STARTED WITH GODOT


I recently start Godot back in March 2026. As I have recently got a new laptop to start game development. I started watching YouTube tutorials on how to use the Godot engine. This is mostly a prototyping and getting a hand of the engine in Godot. I do not know how to add camera movements. Nor how to make a decent UI. But I have learned a lot in Godot and GDScript which helped me familiarize with the engine. It looks simple, and my first stepping stone when I was a newbie. Looking back to my projects now. I'd say I am proud of how I have come and progress.

The sprites are all drawn by me with the use of pixilart.com website. I have learned how to draw sprites, animate it, and even make simple tiles.

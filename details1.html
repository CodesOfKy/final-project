2D Platformer


This project was my refined knowledge to GDScript. I focused on creating smooth character movement and tilemap collisions. The text in the beginning changes depending on how the player dies in the game. There is also a golden apple counter that resets everytime you die. This is mostly a remake to the first platform games I have created. I'd say I am happy on how my progress went throught the weeks or months.

The sprites are all drawn by me with the use of pixilart.com website. This helped me familiarize how to draw in pixels and how to be quicker and efficient when drawing it.
